The game

Play your way.
Make your mark.

KARZEN is an independent game project focused on multiplayer gameplay, player expression and accessible fun. The vision is to bring players together, whatever device they play on.

🌐

Multiplayer first

Room-based play and matchmaking concepts designed to help friends get into a match together.

📱

PC + Android vision

A cross-platform direction so desktop and mobile players can share the same game world.

🎨

Make it yours

Distinct character, vehicle and equipment cosmetics give every player their own look.
