The game

Play your way.
Make your mark.

KARZEN is an independent battle royale focused on multiplayer action, player expression and accessible fun. The vision is to bring players together, whatever device they play on.

🌐

Battle royale first

A big map with houses, named areas and a central factory, built for loot runs, ambushes and last-squad showdowns.

📱

PC + Android vision

A cross-platform direction so desktop and mobile players can share the same game world.

🎨

Make it yours

Outfits, helmets, backpacks, vehicle wraps and weapon skins give every player their own look.
