About KARZEN

KARZEN is a battle royale game project being developed for PC and Android. This website provides information about the game and its privacy practices.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Google Sign-In

KARZEN offers Google Sign-In so players can authenticate using their Google Account. When you choose this option, Google may process your information according to Google's privacy policy and the permissions shown during sign-in.

Information and Use

The information made available through Google Sign-In is used only as needed for authentication and the game features that are actually implemented. The game should not request access to unrelated Google data.

Storage and Sharing

The way information is stored depends on the features enabled in the game. This policy does not promise that information is stored only on your device. KARZEN does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Google to provide its sign-in service.

Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect account-related information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the contact email provided on the KARZEN Google sign-in consent screen.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the game's features or information practices change.
